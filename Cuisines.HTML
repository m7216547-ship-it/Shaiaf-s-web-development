Indian Food

Indian cuisine is a diverse mix of regional styles, defined by distinct local spices, grains, and cooking methods. Staples include rice, whole-wheat flatbreads, lentils, and rich vegetable or meat curries. Famous dishes range from North Indian butter chicken to South Indian masala dosa.

Bengali Food

Bengali cuisine is famous for its delicious taste, abundance of fish and rice, and use of unique spices. In addition to the staple food of rice and freshwater fish, there are vegetables, meat, and various types of sweets and pithas.

Chinese Food

Chinese cuisine is one of the world's oldest and most diverse culinary traditions. It is divided into the famous "Eight Great Traditions," featuring distinct regional flavors ranging from the fiery, numbing spices of Sichuan to the fresh, delicate seafood of Cantonese cooking.

Japanese Food

Japanese cuisine focuses on fresh, seasonal ingredients, natural flavors, and beautiful presentation. Rice and noodles form the base of the diet, complemented by seafood, pickled items, and fermented ingredients like miso and soy sauce.

Korean Food

Korean cusine is of bold flavors, vibrant colors, and fermented ingredients like kimchi. Key staples include rice, vegetables, meats, and spicy pastes like gochujang. Meals often come with small side dishes called banchan.

Malaysian Food

Malaysian cuisine is a rich mix of Malay, Chinese, and Indian tastes. Key dishes include nasi lemak, laksa and roti canai, featuring bold spices like chili, lemongrass, and coconut milk.
